LinkedIn content strategy for Synetech

Synetech · Social Media & Content Creator · April to July 2026 · Madrid

Synetech sells interactive classroom technology in Spain, to wholesalers, distributors and integrators, and to the IT coordinators who choose it for a school. I owned social and content for the brand. The work below comes from the digital strategy I wrote for them, in Spanish.

Benchmarking the site against four competitors

I put Synetech's website next to Newline, Smarttech, i3 Connect and Promethean and scored each one on four things: what sits above the fold, the menu and structure, the content on offer, and the E-E-A-T signals. Scoring every cell rather than writing impressions is what makes the gaps arguable instead of a matter of taste.

The two weakest areas came out as navigation and trust. No product photos in the menu, element names written in internal language rather than the customer's, and no case studies or tutorials, where every competitor had both. Promethean categorised its menu by sector, Smarttech put photos in it, and all four led with customer stories that Synetech did not have.

What the sector posts on LinkedIn, and what we posted

For LinkedIn I coded a sample of the sector's posts by content category and by format, then set Synetech's own mix against the sector average, so the question stopped being what we should post and became where we were over and under weight.

The recommendation was to move output out of HR and events and into product and customer stories, and into the formats the sector was not using much yet, carousels and video, rather than competing on the ones it was saturating.

Working in Spanish

The strategy deck, the analysis and most of the content were written in Spanish. Some posts went out in English where the audience was international, like the German trade fair. This is the clearest evidence I have that working in Spanish is not a problem for me.
