Relaunching Publicis Groupe Hungary on Instagram

Publicis Groupe Hungary · Junior Digital Strategist · August 2024 to August 2025

The agency's own Instagram account was posting about three times a month. There was no video at all in Q4 2024, no consistent design, and the content leaned on a few repeated formats.

Where the strategy came from

Before writing anything I built a database in Excel of every post from our four closest competitors, coded by content pillar, content type, format and posting date, and recorded the performance of each one against likes, comments, saves and shares. That gave me a view of which formats and which topics their audiences actually responded to, rather than an impression picked up from scrolling their feeds.

Two pillars

What We Do covers the agency's work: creative output, case studies, awards, expert insight, interviews, industry data and recent campaigns. Who We Are covers the inside of the agency: the team, the culture, trends and memes, office pets, DEI. Each pillar got a fixed set of recurring formats, so there was always a next post to make rather than a blank page every week.

What changed

Posting went from three a month to eight, with scheduled video every month, new content types, a unified design across posts, highlights, stickers and GIFs, and the newer Instagram features the account had not been using.

New content types

The Last 5 is a series where an employee walks through the last five posts they saved on Instagram. Work Your World profiles people who moved abroad within the group. Expert Insights turns internal data and research into carousels. Publicis Pets is what it sounds like, and it outperformed most of the serious content.

Results

Per post, Q4 2024 against January to April 2025

+57%average views
+388%average shares
+105%average saves
+16%average likes

Against the competitive set

Engagement rate reached 4.31%, the highest of the five agency accounts tracked. The next closest was 3.16%.
