About

I have moved between an agency, a B2B company and an early stage product inside three years, and each of them taught me a different part of the job. Publicis was where I learned how a campaign gets planned, bought and measured across five markets, and then how a strategy gets built out of the data sitting underneath it, which is a slower and less glamorous process than it sounds. Synetech was a reminder that B2B content runs on different rules, because nobody shares a post about enterprise software for fun.

AirHint is the first place where the whole funnel has been mine, and where I had to learn enough about statistics and experiments to trust my own results. Alongside all of that I taught maths on Saturdays for five years, and I build websites because I want to understand how one goes up from nothing, which is also why I keep starting projects of my own.

The thing holding all of it together is curiosity. I want to understand how things work, the whole structure of them, companies, tools and products, all the way down. Then I want to use that to build better products for the people who use them, through marketing.
